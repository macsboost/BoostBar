## Description
BoostBar is a tiny, fast menu bar monitor for your Mac.

NETWORK SPEED
• Live upload/download graph with KB/s or Kb/s readout
• Pick your own trace colors
• Hover for a 5-minute chart and data totals

BATTERY POWER
• See exactly how many watts are flowing into or out of your battery
• Hover for time-to-full or time-to-empty, plus a 10-minute power chart
• On Apple silicon, see what your charger is supplying and what your Mac is using

DISK ACTIVITY LIGHTS
• An LED for each drive that blinks with every read and write, like the classic hard-drive light
• Works with internal SSDs, external drives, USB flash drives and SD cards
• Choose each drive's LED color and blink length (default 35 ms)
• Hover a light to see volumes, free space and live read/write speed

KEEP AWAKE
• One click keeps your Mac from sleeping, indefinitely or for 15 minutes to 8 hours
• Optionally keep the display on too
• The googly eyes go bloodshot while your Mac is being kept awake

ALSO
• Launch at login
• Turn each readout on or off
• Lightweight native app: no Dock icon, minimal CPU

BoostBar is energydrinkware: free to use. If you like it, you can buy the developer an energy drink from the About window.

## Notes for App Review
> BoostBar is a menu bar–only app (LSUIElement); it has no Dock icon and no main window.
> Click the gauge/graph in the menu bar to open its menu.
>
> • Network, battery and disk readouts use public sysctl/IOKit registry reads. No elevated privileges and no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission are used.
> • Disk lights: "Disk Lights…" in the menu lists drives; plug in a USB flash drive to see it appear.
> • Keep Awake uses IOPMAssertionCreateWithName.
> • Hover over any readout in the menu bar to see a detailed chart.
> • The About window ("About BoostBar…" in the menu) contains an optional consumable tip IAP (com.macsboost.boostbar.energydrink) that unlocks nothing.
> • Once per app version the app sends an anonymous install count (random app-generated ID, app version, macOS version, CPU type) to biz-cord.com. This is disclosed in the About window, can be disabled from the menu, and is declared in App Privacy.

# Privacy Policy: BoostBar (publish at macsboost.com/boostbar-privacy)

**Effective:** November 22, 2026 · **Developer:** Macsboost Incorporated (mac@supermac.net)

BoostBar runs entirely on your Mac. Network, battery and disk statistics are read and displayed locally and are never sent anywhere.

**Install count.** Once per app version, BoostBar sends a single message to biz-cord.com containing: a random identifier generated by the app (not tied to you, your Apple ID or your hardware), the BoostBar version, the macOS version, and the processor type. It is used only to count installs and versions. It contains no name, email, location, IP-based profile, file names or usage content. You can turn it off from the BoostBar menu (**Share Anonymous Install Count**). Server logs may briefly record the connecting IP address, as with any web request; it is not linked to the install ID or used to identify you.

**Purchases.** Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

**No tracking, no ads, no selling of data.** BoostBar does not track you across apps or websites.

**Children.** BoostBar is not directed at children and collects no personal information.
